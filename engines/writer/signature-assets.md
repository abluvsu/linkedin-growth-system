# Signature Assets

> **Pipeline Position:** Layer 0 — alongside the Insight Vault, this is the foundation of your content identity.
> **Purpose:** Your reusable intellectual property (frameworks and concepts). These become recognizable mental models associated with your name.
> **Visual Rule:** Whenever possible, these assets should be published alongside a visual (architecture diagram, workflow map, before/after process diagram).

---

## Asset Type 1: Named Frameworks

> Structured mental models showing how to build or execute. Often visual.

### 1. The Execution Stack
**Core Concept:** A 5-layer hierarchy of how a business actually automates. 
**The Stack:** Vision → Process → Automation → AI → Measurement
**When to Use:** When critiquing companies that buy AI tools before fixing their underlying processes. You can't put AI on top of a broken process.

### 2. The AI Adoption Pyramid
**Core Concept:** The steps required to deploy AI in an enterprise successfully.
**The Pyramid:** Data (Foundation) → Workflow (Integration) → Context (RAG/Prompting) → Automation (Agents) → Autonomy (System).
**When to Use:** When advising founders on how to sell AI to enterprise, or when explaining why simple RAG wrappers fail.

### 3. The Operational Chaos Index
**Core Concept:** A diagnostic tool to measure how broken a business process is.
**The Index Elements:** Manual work → Repeated decisions → Disconnected tools → Missing ownership → Poor visibility.
**When to Use:** When breaking down a "System Breakdown" (Monday Post) to diagnose exactly why the company is bleeding money.

## Asset Type 2: Signature Lenses

> A unique way of seeing a common problem. Lenses tell you how to SEE.

### 1. The "Toys vs Tools" Lens (The 60% Rule)
**What It Reveals:** If an AI implementation doesn't reduce manual effort by at least 60%, it's a toy, not a tool.
**When to Use:** As a brutal benchmark for evaluating AI products and agent architectures.

### 2. The "HitL" (Human-in-the-Loop) Lens
**What It Reveals:** Reveals that full AI autonomy in regulated industries (healthcare/insurance) is a liability, not a feature.
**The Reframing:** Moves the conversation from "AI will replace doctors/underwriters" to "AI handles the administration; humans handle the stakes."
**Key Phrase:** "Let me know when you tell the family their ₹2 crore claim is rejected by a machine."

### 3. The "Execution Over Ideation" Lens
**What It Reveals:** Startups focus too much on having the perfect model instead of having the perfect execution engine.
**The Reframing:** "Businesses don't have an AI problem. They have an execution problem."

> Short phrases that become associated with your voice.
