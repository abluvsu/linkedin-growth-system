**Date:** July 15, 2026
**Hook:** "You applied to 50 jobs manually. You got zero callbacks. Using AI to automate that broken strategy will not save you."
**Pillar:** Building AI Products (CareerFlow case study)
**Positioning Lens:** Contrarian (AI automation is bad advice)
**Narrative Archetype:** Contrarian (Popular belief → Challenge → Evidence → Solution)
**ICP Target:** Job Seekers (Primary), Founders/Operators (Secondary)

#### Performance Metrics
- **Views (Impressions):** 24,403
- **Comments:** 107 (Driven by "access code" CTA)
- **Saves:** 75
- **Reactions:** 59
- **Profile Visits:** 151
- **Followers Gained:** 75

#### Audience Demographics
- **Seniority:** Senior (29%), Entry (27%), Manager (5%)
- **Top Titles:** Software Engineer (7%), Product Manager (4%), Data Scientist (3%)

#### Pattern Notes & Learnings
- **The "Save > Reaction" Anomaly:** The post had 75 saves but only 59 reactions. This is the hallmark of a high-utility Lead Magnet. People don't always "like" a post about their job struggles, but they *will* save it for the practical utility (the 3 rules).
- **Profile Conversion:** 151 profile views and 75 followers gained is an extremely high conversion rate from a single post. It proves the hook + CV attachment combination establishes massive authority.
- **Audience Capture:** While targeted at Job Seekers, 29% of viewers were "Senior" and 5% were "Managers". The thesis ("stop writing for robots") resonated up the chain.

### Post 2: Bot vs Bot (The Python Chart Post)
**Date:** July 21, 2026
**Hook:** "Mass-applying with AI is destroying your career. (I have the data on 5,000 resumes to prove it.)"
**Pillar:** Building AI Products (CareerFlow data pull)
**Positioning Lens:** Contrarian / Data-Backed
**Narrative Archetype:** Observation → Pattern → Rule
**ICP Target:** Job Seekers

#### Performance Metrics (3-Hour Snapshot)
- **Views (Impressions):** 130
- **Comments:** Unknown (Likely 0)
- **Engagement Rate:** Flatlined

#### What Failed (The Post-Mortem)
1. **Algorithm Shadowban (Engagement Bait):** 130 views in 3 hours is not a bad post; it's a dead post. The algorithm killed it in the crib. Why? The CTA: *"1. Follow me (so I can DM you) 2. Comment 'TEMPLATE' below."* LinkedIn's 2025/2026 algorithm aggressively penalizes explicit "Follow me" and "Comment [Word]" phrasing in the main body. 
2. **The Hook Was Too "Marketer":** *"I have the data on 5,000 resumes to prove it"* sounds like a B2B SaaS whitepaper pitch. Contrast this with the viral July 15 post: *"You applied to 50 jobs manually. You got zero callbacks."* That is a visceral, human action. The new hook was an abstract flex.
3. **The Image Trap:** We attached a Python-generated chart. LinkedIn often suppresses graphics that look like infographics or ads unless they are native carousels.

#### Action Items for Pipeline
- **[ ] Update Style Guide:** Banish "Follow me" and "Comment [Word]" from main post bodies. Move all engagement mechanisms to organic closing questions or native carousels.
- **[ ] Hook Rule:** Stop flexing data in the hook. Put the human pain in the hook, put the data in paragraph 3.
- **[ ] Format Shift:** Delete the post and reformat the data into a native PDF carousel (not a static chart image). Carousels bypass the static image penalty and increase dwell time.
